Training Methodology

The public MIKE‑CORE training method: reproducible steps, ecosystemic thinking and stability as the foundation for growth — regardless of background, level or starting position.

This page describes the public training approach. Internal methodologies, validation chains and domain structures remain protected.
Why this methodology exists

Training within MIKE‑CORE is not a classic education model. It is an ecosystemic approach that protects people from chaos, pressure and uncertainty. The methodology is designed to build stability — not just knowledge.

Focus
Stability over knowledge

Knowledge without stability breaks people. MIKE‑CORE begins with structure, language and reproducible steps.

Goal
Anyone can start

The method works for youth, professionals and vulnerable groups — without diploma barriers.

Approach
Ecosystemic learning

Problems are not viewed in isolation but in full context: person, process, hardware and environment.

The 4 building blocks of MIKE‑CORE training

The training method consists of four reproducible building blocks that can be applied in any context.

Block 1
Language & Understanding

Participants receive a shared vocabulary: stability, drift, ecosystemic pressure, reproducibility.

Block 2
Structure & Safety

A stable environment where mistakes are not endpoints but signals in the system.

Block 3
Practical steps

Small, achievable assignments that are reproducible and build confidence.

Block 4
Ecosystemic reflection

Not “what did you do wrong?”, but “what in the system caused this behaviour?”.

The MIKE‑CORE training cycle

The training cycle is designed to start small and grow reproducibly.

Phase 1 — Foundation
Language & stability

Participants learn the basic concepts and receive a stable environment to begin.

Phase 2 — Application
Small assignments

Practical exercises around hardware, ecosystemic thinking and reproducible steps.

Phase 3 — Reflection
Ecosystemic analysis

Participants learn to look at context, pressure, expectations and system faults.

Phase 4 — Growth
Scaling what works

What works is captured and repeated. Participants grow through stability, not pressure.
