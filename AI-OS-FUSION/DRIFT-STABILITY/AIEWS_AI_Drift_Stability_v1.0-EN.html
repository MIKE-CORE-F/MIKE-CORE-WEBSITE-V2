Drift & Stability

Drift & Stability within AI‑OS‑FUSION describes how the system detects deviations, maintains stability, and prevents AI modules from evolving outside their ecosystem boundaries. The goal is to ensure predictability, safety, and reproducibility.

What Is AI Drift?

AI Drift occurs when a system deviates from its original context, objectives, or doctrine. This may happen due to:

  • misinterpretation of data
  • accumulation of small deviations
  • incorrect feedback loops
  • unconscious human input
  • incorrect coupling between modules
How AI‑OS‑FUSION Detects Drift

AI‑OS‑FUSION uses context layers, validation points, and ecosystem boundaries to detect deviations immediately. The system continuously compares:

  • current context vs. original context
  • objectives vs. output
  • module behaviour vs. doctrine
  • human input vs. system response
Stability Within MIKE‑CORE

Stability ensures the ecosystem remains predictable, safe, and reproducible. AI‑OS‑FUSION monitors:

  • ecosystem boundaries
  • module interactions
  • drift‑sensitive zones
  • audit trails
  • human intervention points
Why Drift & Stability Are Essential

Without drift control, chaos emerges. Without stability, an IT ecosystem eventually collapses. AI‑OS‑FUSION prevents both by continuously monitoring, correcting, and protecting.
