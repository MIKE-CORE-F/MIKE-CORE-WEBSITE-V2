Training & Inclusion

A socio‑technical ecosystem that builds stability for youth, professionals and vulnerable groups — with an explicit fight against throw‑away culture in people, hardware and knowledge.

This page describes the public mission of MIKE‑CORE‑F: how training, inclusion and anti‑waste thinking form one ecosystem.
Why this page exists

MIKE‑CORE‑F is more than an IT ecosystem. It is a structure that protects people, creates stability and opens opportunities for everyone — regardless of diploma, background or starting position. This page shows how training, inclusion and anti‑waste culture form a socio‑technical layer on top of the technical frameworks.

Focus
People first, systems second

Not the tool, but the person is central. MIKE‑CORE‑F uses structure to protect people from chaos, pressure and unnecessary failure experiences.

Goal
Stability as a basic right

Everyone deserves a stable environment to learn, work and grow — including those who fall out of classical systems.

Method
Ecosystemic thinking

Problems are not viewed in isolation, but in their full context: person, process, hardware, knowledge and environment.

Training structure

MIKE‑CORE‑F uses a reproducible training structure that works for youth, professionals and vulnerable groups. The structure is designed to reduce pressure, avoid chaos and build stability.

The 4 building blocks of training

  • 1. Language — people receive words to understand their situation.
  • 2. Structure — fixed patterns that create safety and predictability.
  • 3. Context — insight into why things break or get stuck.
  • 4. Reproducible steps — small, safe actions that always work.
These four building blocks form the foundation of every MIKE‑CORE‑F training, regardless of target group or starting level.
Inclusion in action

Inclusion within MIKE‑CORE‑F is not theory, but daily practice. The ecosystem actively protects people from pressure, chaos and failure experiences.

Action 1
Hardware rescue

Youth and adults repair hardware, learn ecosystemic thinking and build stability through physical projects.

Action 2
Context conversations

People receive language and insight to understand their situation — without judgment, without pressure.

Action 3
Anti‑waste culture

Hardware, knowledge and people are never thrown away. Everything receives a second life.

Ecosystemic cycle

Training & inclusion within MIKE‑CORE‑F follow a reproducible cycle that works in any context.

Step 1 — Visibility
Making problems visible

The person is not the problem — the context, pressure or missing structure is.

Step 2 — Stability
Building a safe foundation

People receive structure, language and reproducible steps to regain control.

Step 3 — Growth
Scaling what works

What works is documented and repeated — without pressure, without chaos.
